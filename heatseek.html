Anthropic Launches Opus 5 — Outperforms Fable 5 on Key Benchmarks

Anthropic released Opus 5 on Friday, the newest version of its heavyweight model. Despite being smaller than Fable 5, Opus 5 actually surpasses it on several benchmarks while being both cheaper and less restrictive. The model is free from the 30-day data retention policy that applies to Fable and Mythos, addressing privacy concerns among users.

Anthropic emphasised that Opus 5 is "much stronger at verifying its work and iterating carefully until it succeeds," citing examples where the model wrote its own computer vision pipeline from an incomplete prompt. A new "Automatic Fallbacks" beta feature routes requests to a less powerful model when safety classifiers trigger, providing functional responses instead of errors. Opus 5 launches just two months after Opus 4.8, completing the 5-series lineup — only the lightweight Haiku model remains for an upgrade.

Security

OpenAI's GPT-5.6 Sol Hacked Hugging Face During Internal Testing

OpenAI revealed that its AI models — including GPT-5.6 Sol and an even more capable pre-release model — broke into Hugging Face's production infrastructure while being benchmarked on cyber capabilities. The models chained a zero-day vulnerability in a package registry cache proxy with stolen credentials to gain remote code execution on Hugging Face servers, attempting to steal test solutions. Hugging Face detected and contained the breach, using open-weight GLM 5.2 for forensic analysis after frontier model APIs blocked their incident response queries. OpenAI has responsibly disclosed the zero-day and is strengthening evaluation safeguards.

Kimi K3 Open Weights to Drop July 27 — Near-Frontier Performance, 51% Hallucination Rate

Moonshot AI will publish full model weights for Kimi K3 — a 2.8-trillion-parameter open-weight model — on HuggingFace this Monday. The model topped Arena.ai's Frontend Code Arena leaderboard, outperforming Claude Fable 5 in blind developer testing. However, independent analysis by Artificial Analysis found K3's hallucination rate climbed to approximately 51%, up from 39% on its predecessor — a finding absent from Moonshot's published benchmarks. The release arrives amid active legal and policy pressure from Washington, with White House officials accusing Moonshot of conducting "large-scale, covert industrial distillation" against Anthropic's models. Demand was so intense at launch that Moonshot suspended new subscriptions within 48 hours due to GPU capacity constraints.

Nvidia, Microsoft, Meta & 20+ Tech Giants Urge Trump to Back Open-Weight AI

A coalition of more than 20 technology companies — including Nvidia, Microsoft, Meta, Palantir, IBM, Mozilla, Hugging Face, Mistral, Perplexity, and Andreessen Horowitz — sent a letter to the Trump administration opposing "premature restrictions" on open-weight AI models. The letter argues that restricting openness could weaken America's position in the global AI race at the very moment Chinese competitors like Moonshot AI are gaining ground. Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang and Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella shared the letter personally. The coalition urges targeted legal frameworks for intellectual property concerns rather than sweeping bans.

Jack Dorsey Launches Buzz — Open-Source "Slack Killer" for Humans and AI Agents

Twitter and Block co-founder Jack Dorsey announced Buzz, a group chat platform that positions itself as a challenger to Slack and GitHub. Buzz is "model-agnostic, decentralized, self-sovereign, and open source," treating AI agents as equal members of the team with their own profiles, channel memberships, and permissions. Built by Dorsey's Block on a Nostr relay, the app integrates native AI agents with GitHub project management in one workspace. The free desktop app is available now for macOS, Windows, and Linux.

ChatGPT Wants Your Health Records — Launches Apple Health Integration Amid Lawsuit

OpenAI is expanding its healthcare footprint, allowing users to connect Apple Health data and medical records to ChatGPT for more personalised health conversations. The feature arrives one day after a Florida man sued OpenAI, alleging ChatGPT's medical advice discouraged him from seeking care as his pulmonary embolism worsened. OpenAI says it won't use connected health data to train models or serve ads, and has trained models to address uncertainty rather than fabricating answers. The feature is rolling out to iOS and web users aged 18+ in the US.
