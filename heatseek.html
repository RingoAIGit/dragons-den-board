AI Security

The First Attack Where Nobody Was Driving

Google's threat intelligence team says Russian-speaking hackers built a chain of AI agents that found software flaws and wrote working exploits with no human in the loop — the first documented fully automated cyberattack. The agents sat on a staging server running something called exploit_agent, comparing code before and after each patch and firing at whatever was newly exposed. Targets were French media, entertainment and education sites. Google calls AI misuse a "critical inflection point" in its latest threat tracker. Until now, even frontier models needed a skilled operator to steer them and clean up the mistakes.
