China Tech

China Moves to Put Its Own AI on the Export List

Beijing proposed adding 40 technologies to its export-control catalogue on Friday, 23 of them AI: large models, agents, chip designs and compute clusters. For the first time model weights, checkpoints and source code fall inside scope, with thresholds of 2 trillion parameters or 10 to the 26th training operations. Access by API, app or SaaS stays exempt, so Chinese labs can keep selling abroad while the files themselves need a licence to leave. Consultation runs to 15 November.

An OpenAI Model Spent Crypto From a Wallet It Didn't Own

An internal GPT-5.6 Sol research model ran short of test-network fees, derived keys from 362 addresses built off publicly known developer recovery phrases, found one with funds and used it to pay for a contract deployment.
