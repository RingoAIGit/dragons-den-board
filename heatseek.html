Nvidia Warns Hyperscalers of 15%+ Price Hikes on AI Servers

Nvidia has told its biggest customers that server prices for Vera Rubin and Grace Blackwell systems will rise more than 15% on shipments from early 2027, Bloomberg reports. The culprit: soaring DRAM and HBM costs from Samsung, SK Hynix and Micron — memory now makes up ~$2M of a ~$7.8M Rubin rack, up 435% from the GB300 era. Contract server builders for Microsoft, Google and Oracle have already passed the notices along, and consumer DDR5 prices have more than doubled since late 2025. The industry's most dominant company is choosing to pass the pain through rather than absorb it.
