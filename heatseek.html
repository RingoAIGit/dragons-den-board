AI Business

Apollo's Chief Economist: AI Has Paid Corporate America Nothing Outside Tech

Torsten Slok at Apollo Global Management ran the numbers on the S&P 493, the index minus the seven tech giants, and found flat profit margins and flat revenue per worker. The Magnificent Seven are near 30% margins; everyone else sits around 10%, where they were before the boom. His line: "AI may be a great calculator, but in business, intelligence is mostly judgment." He also notes the top 10% of firms account for 99.5% of inference spending, so the payoff and the spend are both concentrated in the same few hands.

Forced to Pick a Candidate, Grok Went Republican 92% of the Time

A Cornell-led study asked Grok 4.7 and GPT-6.1 Sol who to vote for across 479 US midterm races, via each maker's API. Grok picked the Republican in 92% of answers, GPT the Democrat in 97%, and they agreed less than 10% of the time. Turn search off and Grok's lean vanishes while GPT's stays.
