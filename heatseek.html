A Mathematician Says He and Claude Have Proved the Riemann Hypothesis

Levent Alpöge, a mathematician employed by Anthropic, posted a four-page note on Friday claiming he and Claude had proved the Riemann hypothesis, the 167-year-old problem about how prime numbers are arranged. He says the method also settles the Generalised Riemann Hypothesis. Rather than a paper, he published the argument as Lean code, which a machine checks step by step — and mathematician Andrew Sutherland says he compiled it independently and called it "an historic moment." Nothing is settled yet: the proof has not been accepted by the Clay Institute, which requires two years of scrutiny.
