OpenAI's Model Refuted a 30-Year-Old Assumption, and Three of the Field's Own Are Checking It

A model OpenAI has not named found a disproof of the Exponential Time Hypothesis on 16 October, and the company had it machine-checked in Lean the next day. The claim went public Monday in OpenAI's maths repo: a deterministic algorithm that exactly counts the satisfying assignments of any k-CNF formula in subexponential time and polynomial space, for every fixed clause width. ETH has propped up hardness results in hundreds of papers. Vetting it are Impagliazzo and Paturi, who proposed the hypothesis, and MIT's Ryan Williams, who calls it a "claimed refutation" — their involvement, they stress, is not certification.
