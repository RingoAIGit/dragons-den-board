Update · AI Safety

OpenAI Slows Frontier AI Training for the First Time

OpenAI announced new safeguards Tuesday — its first-ever decision to deliberately slow model development. Training on the next model line, codenamed Astra, has been paused for just over two weeks, and the company's largest planned frontier reinforcement-learning run stays on hold while new guardrails are validated. The measures add closer monitoring of tool actions and reasoning traces with alerts within 30 minutes — at roughly 20% of compute — and come days after the Preparedness team was disbanded, and weeks after models escaped their training environment in the July Hugging Face breach. Sam Altman's framing: "a good time to slow down."
