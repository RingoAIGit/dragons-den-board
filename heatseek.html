AI Hardware

OpenAI's Jalapeño Chip Beats Nvidia on Perf-Per-Watt — Taped Out in Just 9 Months

OpenAI unveiled its first custom inference chip, Jalapeño, at Hot Chips — co-developed with Broadcom from a blank slate and taped out in a record ~9 months on TSMC N3P. SemiAnalysis' InferenceX benchmarks, run with OpenAI engineers in the lab, show it beating Nvidia's Blackwell on tokens per watt in almost every scenario — and edging even Vera Rubin on perf-per-MW without speculative decoding, which Rubin relies on. The B0 stepping delivers 13.4 PFLOPs of MXFP4 with HBM4, and engineering samples are already serving models at production frequency. Gigawatt-scale deployments with Microsoft and other partners are slated for the end of 2026, and OpenAI is pitching the internal "Teacup" serving engine plus Codex-written kernels as a full-stack advantage.
