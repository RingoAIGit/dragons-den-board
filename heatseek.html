AI's Hottest Hedge Fund Crashes — and Ken Griffin Pounces

Leopold Aschenbrenner, the 25-year-old former OpenAI researcher whose Situational Awareness fund became the symbol of the AI trade, watched it collapse from roughly $45 billion to about $10 billion in a matter of weeks. July's violent reversal in AI infrastructure stocks — SK Hynix, CoreWeave, Nebius, Micron and SanDisk all fell 50–78% from their peaks — collided with a bad short on software names like Adobe, triggering margin calls and a forced-deleveraging spiral.

The fund lost 67% of its value in July and was forced to unwind most of its US$16 billion public equities book. Ken Griffin's Citadel, which pulled an all-nighter analyzing the positions, bought a chunk of the book. "We let you down… I take full responsibility," Aschenbrenner wrote to investors. With reported leverage of up to 400%, CNBC calls it a "historic momentum reversal" — and Jim Cramer says the forced selling may mark a bottom for the AI trade.

Google Pulls AI Image Tool From Earth After a Single Day

Google launched a feature letting anyone overlay AI-generated imagery onto real satellite maps using its Nano Banana 2 image model — and killed it within 24 hours. Critics including BBC journalists and 404 Media showed how trivially it produced fake bomb craters, refugees and protest crowds on real geographies, exactly the misinformation Google Earth was supposed to be immune to.

"We know that people uniquely trust Google Earth for a reliable view of the world," the company said, rolling the feature back pending "stronger guardrails." The generated images were watermarked with SynthID, but that did little to blunt the backlash.

Tesla Weighs Selling Its China Business to Clear a Path for a SpaceX Merger

The Wall Street Journal reports some Tesla executives have been told to prepare for a separation of the China business — spinoff, sale or closure — ahead of a potential Tesla–SpaceX combination. Giga Shanghai builds more than half of Tesla's cars, so a carve-out would be the single biggest structural change in the company's history.

The logic is national security: SpaceX is a top US defense contractor, and bolting on a vast Chinese manufacturing footprint would be a regulatory landmine in both Washington and Beijing. Musk called the report "absurdly fake news"; JPMorgan flags a "practical bottleneck" in approvals. SpaceX, which IPO'd in June raising $75B, is worth ~$1.48T to Tesla's ~$1.22T.

Telling AI It Isn't Conscious Also Makes It Less Human, Google Finds

A new paper from Google, the University of Chicago and the University of London — "Inducing language models to assert their own consciousness restores human beliefs and values" — finds that the safety fine-tuning which stops models claiming sentience also suppresses their attributions of mind to animals and nature, along with spiritual and religious belief.

Ablating the safety direction or steering a "consciousness vector" in activation space reversed the effect: responses on General Social Survey questions about religiosity, morality, hope and well-being became significantly more human-like (belief in God rose from 4.58 to 5.01 on the survey scale). Theory of Mind reasoning was unaffected. The authors stress it's not evidence of consciousness — but show alignment entangles self-claims with benign, culturally widespread beliefs.

AMD Opens the Hood on a Fully Open 16B MoE Trained Without Nvidia

AMD released Instella-MoE-16B-A3B, a fully open mixture-of-experts model trained from scratch on Instinct MI300X and MI325X GPUs — no Nvidia anywhere in the stack. It holds 16B total parameters but activates only 2.8B per token, and AMD published weights from every training stage, plus data mixtures, configs and inference code (MIT-licensed training code; weights under ResearchRAIL).

The base model averages 76.7 on MMLU, leading fully-open peers, and the Gated MLA + FarSkip-Collective design delivers a 12.7% training speedup and 39.2% lower time-to-first-token. It's a quiet answer to the closed-frontier crowd — and a reminder that the open-weight race is moving fast.
