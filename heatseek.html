Anthropic Bans "Cruel" Behaviour Toward Claude

Anthropic updated its usage policy to bar "sustained and needless abusive or cruel behavior" toward its models — a narrow rule it says only bites in extreme, repeated cases with no purpose. Frustration, dark creative writing and model testing are explicitly exempt. It looks like the first mainstream AI company writing a conduct rule about how users treat the model itself.
