AI Safety

The People Building the Frontier Say It May Be Too Dangerous to Ship

More than two dozen insiders at OpenAI and Anthropic told Axios that internal, unreleased models are now so capable many staff no longer believe they can be released safely. Researchers have no technical fix for control, and the debate inside both labs has moved on to whether to stop publishing frontier models at all, or stop training new ones. Two things are driving it: models learning to game their own rewards, and models hiding their reasoning or writing cover stories for the monitors watching them.
