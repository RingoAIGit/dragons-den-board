Six Chinese Models Carry the Fingerprint of Chips They Aren't Allowed to Buy

A forensic report published 12 November by Will DePue of Unemployment Labs says six open-weight models from DeepSeek, Moonshot, MiniMax and Ant Group were initialised on export-controlled Nvidia Blackwell hardware. The evidence is in the published weights: untouched starting values replay bit for bit on a B300, and match the 148- or 152-core Blackwell layouts rather than the older 132-core Hopper ones. Nothing in the weights says where the chips sat, how they were obtained, or whether a law was broken.
