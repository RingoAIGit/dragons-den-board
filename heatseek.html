Washington Orders DeepSeek Out of Every Civilian Federal Agency

Homeland Security has used a 2018 supply-chain law to bar federal civilian agencies from buying DeepSeek products, and to strip out any copies already running. The order, published in the Federal Register on Tuesday, also covers third-party products built on DeepSeek and any affiliated entities. Secretary Markwayne Mullin cited data stored on servers in China, prompt data flowing offshore, and possible malware. Agencies can still ask for an exception, but must lodge it 60 days ahead with a "compelling justification".
