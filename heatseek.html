Sanders Bill Would Ban Superintelligence - and Jail Executives for 20 Years

Sen. Bernie Sanders and Rep. Greg Casar have introduced the Ban Artificial Superintelligence Act. It would prohibit development of artificial superintelligence - a system the bill says is capable of the "destruction or disempowerment of humanity," including overthrowing the government - and legally pause frontier models trained past a set data threshold. Work could only resume once Congress stands up a scientist-led Department of Artificial Intelligence to police models and "supervise the destruction" of superintelligence. Leaders who break the rules, and rogue actors who are not employed by a lab, would face up to 20 years in prison - a penalty the bill compares to unlawfully developing nuclear weapons.
