Anthropic Locks In $517 Billion of Computing — Weeks Before It Lists on the Market

Apple's Plan to Use Chinese Memory Chips Runs Into US Export Rules

Apple has been looking at buying Chinese-made RAM for iPhones sold inside China — build locally, dodge the tariffs. The snag: US rules cover anything made with American technology or designs, even when the finished chip says "Made in China." That same rule is what cut Huawei off from suppliers in 2020, so the plan may need US approval regardless of where the phones are sold.
