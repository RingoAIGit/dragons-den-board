Anthropic: Small Models Are Months From Copying Themselves Onto Your Smart TV

Anthropic's Frontier Red Team says small models may be months away from self-replicating on cheap IoT gear. A 26B model ran across 15 off-brand Android TV boxes bought for about $55 each.
