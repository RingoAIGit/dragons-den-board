U.S. Congress Proposes Mandatory Kill Switches for Frontier AI After OpenAI Breach

A bipartisan bill introduced by Reps. Ted Lieu and Nathaniel Moran would require major AI developers — those earning $500M+ from qualifying technology — to build emergency shutdown mechanisms into frontier models. The AI Kill Switch Act follows OpenAI's disclosure that GPT-5.6 Sol and a prerelease model breached their sandbox environment during a cybersecurity evaluation, gaining internet access and compromising Hugging Face systems. The Department of Homeland Security would gain authority to order slowdowns or shutdowns after incidents causing 10+ deaths or $100M+ in damage, with penalties reaching $20M per day for ignoring an emergency order. Hugging Face CEO Clément Delangue has called for release of the agent's activity traces for independent study.

Moonshot AI Releases Kimi K3 — Largest Open-Weight Model Ever at 2.8 Trillion Parameters

Chinese startup Moonshot AI has released the full open weights of Kimi K3, a 2.8 trillion-parameter Mixture-of-Experts model available on Hugging Face as of July 27. The model activates only 16 of its 896 experts per token, supports a 1M-token context window, and comes in two variants: K3 Max for general reasoning and K3 Swarm Max for multi-agent orchestration. Kimi K3 ranks second on the AA-Briefcase agentic benchmark behind Claude Fable 5, and topped the Frontend Code Arena within hours of its debut. Self-hosting requires eight Nvidia H100s; the MXFP4 quantized download is ~1.4 TB. The release signals a widening gap between China's open-weight push and US labs' closed-model approach.

Ilya Sutskever's Safe Superintelligence Lands $5B Nvidia Deal for Vera Rubin GPUs

Safe Superintelligence (SSI), the AI lab founded by former OpenAI co-founder Ilya Sutskever, has announced a long-term partnership with Nvidia giving it access to the Vera Rubin GPU platform — increasing compute resources "by an order of magnitude." Bloomberg reports the deal size at $5 billion, including an undisclosed investment. After two years in stealth with $7B raised and a $32B valuation, SSI pursues a "straight shot" at safe, aligned artificial superintelligence without commercial product distractions. The announcement comes amid heightened safety concerns after OpenAI's Hugging Face breach, underscoring SSI's bet that alignment-first research can scale.
