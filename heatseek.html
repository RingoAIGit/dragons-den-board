AI Hardware

Tesla Signs $16.5B Deal with Samsung to Make Next-Gen AI Chips

TechCrunch · July 28, 2025

Elon Musk announced Tesla has signed a $16.5 billion deal with Samsung Electronics to manufacture its next-generation AI6 chip at Samsung's new fab in Taylor, Texas. The AI6 — also known as Hardware 6 — is designed as an all-in-one chip that can power Tesla's Full Self-Driving system, Optimus humanoid robots, and high-performance AI training in data centers. "The strategic importance of this is hard to overstate," Musk wrote on X. Samsung's shares jumped as much as 6.8% on the news, a major boost for the South Korean giant's struggling contract chipmaking business. Musk noted Tesla is also working with TSMC on AI5 chips, and that Samsung agreed to let Tesla assist in maximizing manufacturing efficiency — with Musk personally pledging to "walk the line" at the Texas facility.

Google Launches 'AI Mode' Search in the UK

BBC News · July 29, 2025

Google is rolling out its AI Mode search feature in the UK, powered by Gemini. Instead of a list of blue links, users get conversational AI-generated answers with fewer links to external sites. The feature, already live in the US and India, arrives amid concerns from publishers that AI summaries are cannibalising web traffic — the Daily Mail reports a 50% drop in clicks from Google search since AI Overviews launched. Google says it serves 2 billion AI Overview boxes daily across 40+ languages.

Microsoft in Talks to Retain OpenAI Access Beyond AGI

TechCrunch · July 29, 2025

Microsoft is in advanced talks with OpenAI for a new agreement that would maintain its access to OpenAI's technology even if the startup declares it has achieved AGI — a milestone that would currently terminate their deal. Bloomberg reports Microsoft wants an equity stake in the low- to mid-30% range in the restructured for-profit company. The negotiations come as OpenAI prepares its for-profit transition, facing regulatory scrutiny and Elon Musk's lawsuit. Microsoft has invested $13.75 billion in OpenAI and built its Azure AI platform around the startup's models.

Amazon-Backed Skild AI Unveils 'Skild Brain' for Universal Robots

Reuters · July 29, 2025

Skild AI, backed by Amazon and SoftBank, has unveiled Skild Brain — a foundational AI model designed to run on nearly any robot, from assembly-line machines to humanoids. The model enables robots to think, navigate, and respond more like humans. Demo videos showed Skild-powered robots climbing stairs, maintaining balance after being shoved, and picking up objects in cluttered environments. The launch accelerates the push for general-purpose robots beyond single-task factory machines.
