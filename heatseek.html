AI Business

Google Gives Gemini a Persistent "Coworker" Agent

At its Gemini at Work event, Google Cloud unveiled a universal agent for work — not just an assistant that answers, but one that acts. Each agent gets its own Workspace identity: an email address, calendar, Drive and a spot in the company directory at @agents.company.com, and it "acts under its own identity rather than yours." It can run for hours or days, spin up rosters of temporary sub-agents, respond to events, and carry the same memory across devices. By default it picks the best model for the job — including Anthropic's Claude — and works on Microsoft 365 and Slack, not just Google's own apps. Gemini now claims 1bn+ monthly users.

Google Opens Its AI Watermark Checker to Everyone

Google's SynthID Detector is now public: upload an image, video or audio file and it flags the invisible watermarks it embeds, across its own models and partners OpenAI, Nvidia and Kakao (Apple coming). Google says it has watermarked 180bn+ images and videos plus 240,000 years of audio. Caveats worth knowing: it only finds SynthID marks — plenty of AI content carries none — and you must sign in, with roughly 10 checks a day.
