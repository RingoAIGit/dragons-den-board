Databricks Closes $5B Round at a $190B Valuation — After Investors Threw $15B at It

Databricks said Thursday it closed a $5 billion funding round at a $190 billion valuation — up 42% from February's $134 billion mark and above the $188 billion floated in July. CEO Ali Ghodsi told TechCrunch the company had wanted to raise just $1 billion, but interest hit $15 billion after the press got wind of the raise, so it issued more stock and let roughly two dozen VCs in. The round was led by Coatue, with Blackstone, MGX, T. Rowe Price and new investor Sixth Street Growth. The company also crossed a $7 billion revenue run-rate, up more than 80% year over year, and will pour capital into its Lakebase, Genie and Unity AI Gateway products. Six months after its last raise, Databricks keeps choosing private capital over an IPO.

US Senator Urges Trump to Incentivize American Open-Weight AI — Warning of China's 'Huawei Playbook'

Sen. Jim Banks (R-Ind.) asked the Trump administration to devise incentives for US companies to build open-weight AI models, warning that cheap Chinese models could "burrow into the global economy only to be weaponized, like rare earths, at a time and place of China's choosing." In a letter to economic adviser Christopher Phelan, the Armed Services Committee member also pushed for tougher action on illicit chip acquisition and industrial-scale distillation, and requested a staff-level briefing within 60 days. The letter lands in the middle of a split industry debate: Nvidia and Meta urged policymakers in July not to restrict open weights, while Anthropic's Dario Amodei has argued they pose security risks.

Alibaba Opens Up Qwen 3.8 — a 27B Vision Model With 262K Context Under Apache 2.0

Qwen released open weights for Qwen3.8-27B, a 27-billion-parameter multimodal dense model that the team says outperforms the larger Qwen3.7-Plus on coding and office benchmarks. It natively handles 262K tokens of context (extendable to 1M via YaRN), understands images and multi-hour video, and ships with flexible thinking modes. The FP8 variant scores 61.7 on SWE-Bench Pro, 73.0 on Terminal-Bench 2.1 and 90.3 on LiveCodeBench v6. Max-level Qwen3.8-2.4T-A95B weights also landed, and the Hugging Face thread hit 1,090 points within a day — the latest salvo in the open-weight race between US and Chinese labs.
