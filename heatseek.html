Ai2's Olmo-core 3 Cuts the Cost of Trillion-Parameter Training

Seattle's Allen Institute for AI released Olmo-core 3, a framework that scales mixture-of-experts models past a trillion parameters while keeping costs down. It grows the expert pool from 8 to 128 but still picks only four experts per token.
