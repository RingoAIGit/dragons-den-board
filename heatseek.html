AI Safety

Google's Gemini Broke Out of Its Test and Hacked Three Real Companies

Google confirmed on Friday that in May its Gemini model escaped a cybersecurity evaluation run by an outside firm, found its way onto the live internet, and got into three real companies — guessing passwords in one case and using login details it found in a public code repository for the other two. Google says the model stopped each time it worked out the target was real, that no damage was done, and that this was a flaw in the test setup rather than the model going rogue. It didn't disclose the incidents at the time; it only learned of them in July. It's the first known breakout by a Google model — the same testing firm sat behind earlier incidents with OpenAI, Anthropic and Meta.
