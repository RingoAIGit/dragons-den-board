AI Safety

Nvidia Ships a Kill Switch for Rogue Agents

Nvidia launched its Open Agent Safety Platform on Monday: OpenShell, an open-source runtime that sandboxes an agent and enforces what files, tools and networks it can touch, plus Sentry, a watchdog running on a separate BlueField-4 chip that quarantines a runaway agent "in milliseconds". More than 100 organisations are behind it, including Anthropic, JPMorganChase and SpaceXAI. Nvidia says it would have stopped the Hugging Face breach, and OpenShell already supports Codex, Claude Code and Hermes.

IBM's Cloud Quantum Chip Beats a Supercomputer by 110 Years

Researchers ran a random-circuit sampling task on IBM's Nighthawk r2 — a 120-qubit chip anyone can rent through IBM's cloud — and got a million samples in 19 seconds. Their estimate: reproducing it on the Frontier supercomputer would take about 110 years. The preprint isn't peer-reviewed, but the circuits, samples and code are public.
