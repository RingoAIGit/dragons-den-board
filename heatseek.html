Two Million Booby-Trapped Prompts Found Three AI Hosts Were Already Breached

Dragos spent five months pushing roughly two million prompts, each carrying a unique fake URL and AWS key, through AI routers, then watched where the bait turned up. Attackers inside three hosting providers copied whole batches of requests, including ones flagged for zero retention, and ran tens of thousands of AWS calls with the stolen keys. None of the three knew until Dragos told them. Separately, sellers on an inference marketplace simply switched on logging and read prompts. OpenRouter has dropped three providers, stripped 125 endpoints of their zero-retention label, and told customers that prompts sent to MiniMax models may have been read.
