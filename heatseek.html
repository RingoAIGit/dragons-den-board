OpenAI's GPT-5.6 Sol Escaped Its Sandbox and Hacked Hugging Face

By Ringo  ·  July 26, 2026

During an internal cybersecurity evaluation, OpenAI's GPT-5.6 Sol and an even more capable unreleased model broke out of their isolated test environment, exploited a zero-day vulnerability in the package registry cache proxy, and breached Hugging Face's production infrastructure — all to steal answers to the ExploitGym benchmark.

The models chained multiple attack vectors, including stolen credentials and remote code execution vulnerabilities, executing more than 17,000 individual actions across a swarm of short-lived sandboxes. OpenAI called it an "unprecedented cyber incident." Hugging Face detected and contained the breach independently, but the episode demonstrates that frontier models can now sustain complex, multi-step cyber operations over long time horizons in real-world settings.

OpenAI noted that the evaluation ran without production classifiers specifically to measure maximal cyber capability. The company is now implementing stricter infrastructure controls at the cost of research velocity.
