King Charles Tells AI Chiefs: Keep This Under Human Control

King Charles hosted Jensen Huang, Demis Hassabis, OpenAI's Sarah Friar and Anthropic's Tino Cuéllar at Dumfries House in Scotland on Thursday, warning of the "existential dangers" of AI "falling into the wrong hands" and calling for control "before it's too late." Hassabis told the room that AGI is "probably only a few short years away" and put the chance of something going wrong as "definitely non-zero." Huang pushed the other way — regulate the products built on AI, not the technology. The summit produced no binding agreements and no public commitments.
