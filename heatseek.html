Claude Just Ran an Entire Protein-Design Lab — and Beat the Field

Anthropic handed Claude (Mythos Preview and Opus 4.8) a protocol prompt, GPUs and open-source protein-design tools — and it ran the whole campaign itself: picking epitopes, installing software, optimizing and ranking 1,320 designs across 15 targets in 24–48 hours. Independent labs Adaptyv Bio and Twist Bioscience confirmed 354 binders — a 26.8% hit rate against the typical 10–15% — succeeding on 14 of 15 targets. Its best RBX1 binder was ~10x tighter than a competition winner (3.9 nM vs 45 nM). A separate run parsed raw NMR/LC-MS chemistry data in about 20 minutes. Every tool used was open-source, so any lab can now do this.
