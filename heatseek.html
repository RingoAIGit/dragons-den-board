Anthropic Built a Way to Prove Which Model Answered You

Its Frontier Red Team open-sourced FlashProve: a provider commits to a model's weights, then every reply carries a proof a normal CPU checks in about 17 seconds. Claude designed the system, and the security claims are machine-checked in Lean. Tested on a 400B model on one H100 at 110 tokens/sec — roughly 70-95x the GPU cost of just running it.
