Nvidia Acquires Groq Assets in Landmark $20 Billion AI Chip Deal

CNBC · 3 days ago

Nvidia has agreed to acquire assets from AI chip startup Groq for approximately $20 billion in cash — its largest acquisition ever. Groq, whose CEO Jonathan Ross helped invent Google's TPU, will see its founder and senior leaders join Nvidia as part of a "non-exclusive licensing agreement" for Groq's inference technology. The deal comes just months after Groq raised $750 million at a $6.9 billion valuation, and positions Nvidia to integrate Groq's ultra-low-latency LPU chips into its AI factory architecture. "We plan to integrate Groq's low-latency processors into the NVIDIA AI factory architecture," wrote Jensen Huang, "extending the platform to serve an even broader range of AI inference and real-time workloads."

Hermes Agent v2026.7.20 — "The Quicksilver Release"

Nous Research · July 20, 2026 · 2,245 commits · 450+ contributors

Nous Research shipped Hermes Agent v0.19.0, a massive update that slashes first-turn latency by 80% — from ~4.3s down to ~0.9s across CLI, gateway, TUI, desktop, and cron. Reasoning models now stream their thinking live by default, the desktop app received a ~20-PR speed overhaul, and smart approvals become the default — an LLM reviewer independently assesses flagged commands, reducing approval fatigue.
