AI Security

Three Researchers Used Claude to Break Into OpenAI in 72 Hours

A three-person team at cybersecurity firm Hacktron chained two bugs — an unpatched image-parsing flaw in the forum software OpenAI's community site runs on, and a login misconfiguration — to reach ChatGPT and Codex accounts belonging to OpenAI staff, then OpenAI's private code repository. They proved it with a harmless pull request. The whole attack took under 72 hours and, by the team's account, only became possible once Anthropic's Claude Opus 5 shipped: it produced a working exploit in three hours. OpenAI fixed its side within 14 hours and paid a $6,500 bounty. The same underlying image flaw also touches Slack, Meta and GitHub Enterprise.
