Amazon Is Destroying Rare Books to Feed Its AI Training

404 Media tracked a device planted in a rare book to Amazon's Las Vegas facility: the company buys out-of-print texts, cuts off their spines and scans them for model training. Pre-2022 books are prized because nothing published before then was written by an LLM — training on AI output risks "model collapse." Amazon says it buys books "through commercial channels to improve products."
