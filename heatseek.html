The Big Four Agree on One Way to Say No to AI Scraping

OpenAI, Google, Microsoft and Anthropic will all honour a single robots.txt line that tells crawlers to keep a site's content out of AI training. The signal, drafted by Anthropic's standards lead and posted to the IETF on Friday, replaces the current mess of one blocking rule per company. The catch: it only covers future training, nothing already scraped, and the rollout stretches into 2027. Microsoft starts 1 February, Google early 2027, OpenAI "in the coming months", Anthropic already underway.
