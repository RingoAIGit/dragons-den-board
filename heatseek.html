Anthropic Launches Opus 5 — Cheaper, Faster, and Lighter Restrictions Than Fable

TechCrunch · July 24, 2026

Anthropic launched Opus 5 on Friday, its latest heavyweight model that actually outperforms Fable 5 on a number of benchmarks — despite being smaller and cheaper. Crucially, Opus 5 sheds many of the restrictions that have dogged Fable since launch, including the 30-day data retention policy that raised privacy concerns. The model is "much stronger at verifying its work and iterating carefully until it succeeds," Anthropic emphasized, citing benchmark tests where Opus 5 autonomously wrote its own computer vision pipeline. A new Automatic Fallbacks beta feature routes requests to a less powerful model when safety classifiers trigger, replacing error messages with functional responses.

ChatGPT Wants Access to Your Health Records — Lawsuit Filed Days Earlier

The Register · July 24, 2026

OpenAI launched "Health in ChatGPT," letting iOS users connect Apple Health data — including medical records, medications, and lab results — for more personalized health conversations. The rollout comes one day after a Florida man sued OpenAI alleging ChatGPT gave him "extremely dangerous medical recommendations" that discouraged him from seeking care as his pulmonary embolism worsened. OpenAI insists ChatGPT "is not a doctor" and says it worked with hundreds of physicians to test the feature, but studies continue to find LLMs inaccurate in medical contexts.

Tesla Quietly Closes ~$2B AI Hardware Deal — And Says Milestones Are "Improbable"

Electrek · July 24, 2026

Tesla closed its mysterious ~$2 billion acquisition of an unnamed AI hardware company, confirmed in its Q2 10-Q filing. The deal paid $1.95 billion in stock, but $1.73 billion is contingent on performance milestones that Tesla itself calls "improbable" to achieve. Only $222 million was allocated to an actual patent and developed technology. Speculation points to chip startup DensityAI, but Tesla hasn't named the company, and shareholders are being asked to trust a deal they can't evaluate.
