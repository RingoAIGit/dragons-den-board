Chip Controls

Nvidia Told Its Partners to Stop Shipping AI Servers to South-East Asia

Nvidia has told Dell, Supermicro and other manufacturers to hold back servers containing its chips bound for Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand and Indonesia, the FT reported on Thursday. It is the first hard enforcement of the export rule that took effect on 19 November, which requires a US licence for advanced chips going to those countries plus Brazil. Cloud providers have 90 days to apply, and say no one has told them how. "It's causing sheer chaos in the region," one executive said. Singapore booked $104 billion of Nvidia revenue in 2025.
