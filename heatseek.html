Gates: 'Human Reserved' Jobs and an AI Tax — or the 'Worst Source of Injustice'

Bill Gates published a ~6,000-word essay warning the AI transition "will be one of the most turbulent times in human history" and that governments are unprepared. He proposes "Human Reserved" roles — jobs machines could do but society keeps for people, like delivering a terminal diagnosis — plus a tax on AI tokens and robots to rebalance incentives, and new national bodies to coordinate AI policy across security, jobs and health. "AI will either be the greatest equalizer ever invented, or the worst source of injustice," he wrote. Even Gates admits the details need to be "worked out in public."
