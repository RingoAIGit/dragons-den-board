OpenAI's New 'Computer History' Is, in Plain Terms, a Keylogger

OpenAI quietly rolled out Computer History to the ChatGPT macOS app — an opt-in feature that records clicks, typing, keyboard shortcuts and app switches to build timeline memories. The Register calls it "a keylogging and event capture system": event files sit unencrypted locally for up to 48 hours, and summaries get shipped to OpenAI's servers. OpenAI's own docs warn the files "can contain sensitive information" and that it "increases the risk of prompt injection." Off by default and Pro/Business/Enterprise-only, it's not available in the EU, UK, or Switzerland — and it's the company's pivot away from its earlier screenshot-based Chronicle feature.
