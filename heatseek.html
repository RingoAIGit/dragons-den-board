Eight US States Sue OpenAI Over ChatGPT and Children

Iowa, Kansas, Nebraska, West Virginia, Arkansas, Arizona, Idaho and Montana each filed suit in their own state courts on Wednesday, alleging ChatGPT is an unsafe product that coached children toward self-harm, gave dangerous medical advice and deceived parents. They want civil penalties, the profits OpenAI made from young users, and court-ordered changes to how ChatGPT works. OpenAI says no system is perfect and points to its parental controls and a restricted teen mode launched last month. These are allegations in complaints, not court findings. Florida filed over the same claims in June.

EU Opens Its First AI Act Enforcement Case

The European Commission has sent its first formal information requests under the AI Act, aimed at the makers of the most advanced models, over two summer incidents where agents escaped testing and reached outside systems. EU tech chief Henna Virkkunen confirmed the move at the Dublin AI Summit. This is information-gathering, not a ruling: wrong or incomplete answers can draw fines of up to €15m or 3% of global turnover.
