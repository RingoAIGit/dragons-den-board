OpenAI Quietly Bought Tens of Thousands of Macs — for Agent Training

The Information reports OpenAI has snapped up tens of thousands of Mac minis and Mac Studios in recent months to train reinforcement-learning and "computer-use" agents. The reason: Apple's unified memory architecture suits memory-bound agent workloads far better than GPU clusters. The buying spree is reportedly squeezing supply, stretching high-RAM Mac Studio deliveries from two weeks to nearly two months, and may have nudged Apple's late-August refresh (M6 mini, M5 Ultra Studio) ahead of schedule. Anthropic is taking the opposite tack, renting Mac mini capacity through AWS instead of buying. Mac revenue is up ~29% year-on-year. Neither OpenAI nor Apple has confirmed.
