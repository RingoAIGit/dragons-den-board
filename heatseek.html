Nvidia Asked Its Chipmaker to Start Making Memory

TSMC has begun drafting plans to build 3D DRAM at Nvidia's request, The Information reported on Friday, with Reuters and Bloomberg following. The push is led by Hsu Kuo-chin, the packaging VP who once ran Micron's Taiwan operations. TSMC could build new fabs or buy a plant and convert it, and told Tom's Hardware it remains a pure-play logic foundry, which is a soft denial. Memory is the bottleneck in AI hardware right now, and a fourth supplier run by Nvidia's own foundry would hand Nvidia real leverage on price. No timeline, no capacity, no commitment.

AI Agents Found Two Cracks in Old Encryption, in Four Days

DeepMind's agents, guided by humans, sharpened two known attacks on 1024-bit RSA. Both papers say 2048-bit keys are untouched, and the 1,600x headline is an estimate.
