Big Tech

Apple Puts a Touchscreen on the Mac, and a Price Tag to Match

Apple announced its first touchscreen MacBook Pro at a "Pro and tell" event in Los Angeles on Wednesday, ending decades of insisting laptops did not need fingers. The 14-inch starts at US$2,899 and the 16-inch at US$3,399, both a step up from the models they replace, with pre-orders open and stock from 6 November. The panel is a tandem OLED Apple calls PROLED XDR: 2,000 nits, 120Hz, 10,000,000:1 contrast. M5 Pro or M5 Max inside, up to 128GB of memory and 16TB of storage, a redesigned hinge so the screen stays put when you touch it, and the glowing lid logo is back. NZ pricing not announced.
