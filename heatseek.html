🔬 AI

Anthropic Launches Opus 5 — Smaller, Cheaper, Outperforms Fable 5

July 24, 2026 · TechCrunch

Anthropic released Opus 5, the newest heavyweight model that's smaller and cheaper than Fable 5 yet outperforms it on several benchmarks. Unlike Fable, Opus 5 is not subject to the 30-day data retention policy, and its safety classifiers engage 85% less often. A new Automatic Fallbacks beta feature routes flagged requests to a less powerful model instead of returning an error. Opus 5 also demonstrated the ability to write its own computer vision pipeline from an incomplete prompt — a first for the series.

🏥 AI HEALTH

ChatGPT Health Rolls Out to Every US Adult

July 23, 2026 · TechCrunch

OpenAI launched ChatGPT Health to all logged-in US adults, connecting to Apple Health, medical records, One Medical and Function Health. OpenAI's VP of health product claimed models are "now capable of reasoning at levels better than clinician level." Free users get GPT-5.5 Instant; paying users get GPT-5.6 Sol. The launch came days after a Florida pastor sued OpenAI over allegedly dangerous medical advice from the chatbot.

🇨🇳 CHINA AI

Kimi K3 Open Weights Arrive July 27 — 2.8T Parameters, 51% Hallucination Rate

July 24, 2026 · TechTimes

Moonshot AI will publish full weights for Kimi K3, a 2.8-trillion-parameter open-weight model topping Arena.ai's Frontend Code Arena. But independent testing found its hallucination rate climbed from 39% (K2.6) to ~51%. At 1.4 TB in MXFP4 precision, only cloud operators on Blackwell or MI400 hardware can realistically run it. The US has accused Moonshot of conducting "large-scale, covert industrial distillation" against Anthropic's Fable model.

💬 PLATFORMS

Jack Dorsey Launches Buzz — Open Source Slack Challenger with Native AI Agents

July 21, 2026 · TechCrunch

Block released Buzz, a free, open-source collaboration platform built on the Nostr protocol where humans and AI agents work together. Agents have cryptographic identities, defined permissions, and can post, review code, and run automations alongside humans. Model-agnostic and agent-agnostic, Buzz is available at buzz.xyz under Apache-2.0 license, with code on GitHub. Dorsey called it "model-agnostic, decentralized, self-sovereign, and open source."

⚡ HARDWARE

Google's "Frozen v2" AI Chip Promises 6–10× Efficiency for Gemini Inference

July 20, 2026 · TechCrunch

Google is designing "Frozen v2", a specialised inference chip that bakes Gemini's architecture directly into silicon for 6–10× more tokens per unit of power than its latest TPUs. Unlike the shelved original Frozen (which would have fused model weights permanently), Frozen v2 hardwires the architecture while keeping weights loadable. Target is a limited internal rollout in 2028, treating it as a test bed rather than a TPU-scale flagship.

⚖️ COPYRIGHT

Judge Approves Anthropic's $1.5B Copyright Settlement with Authors

July 2026 · AP News

A federal judge approved Anthropic's $1.5 billion settlement with thousands of authors whose pirated books were used to train Claude. Authors receive about $3,000 per book. The settlement resolves a class action that found Anthropic wrongfully acquired millions of books through pirate websites, though training AI on copyrighted material was ruled not illegal in itself.
