Anthropic: Claude Was Used to Write Missile Software and Run State Espionage

Anthropic's first threat-intelligence report of the year, published Thursday, details how its Claude models were used for cyber-espionage, bioweapons-adjacent research and conventional weapons work. It says operators in northern Yemen used Claude to write missile-guidance and flight-control software "in place of human software engineers," and that a Russia-linked group ran an entire espionage campaign with AI workflows. Separate cases involved Chinese university students and three Iranian state-aligned accounts running mass-surveillance and influence campaigns. Anthropic says it blocked every case and has signed an eight-week review agreement with evaluator METR.
