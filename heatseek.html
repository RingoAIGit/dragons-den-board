Meta Told to Pay $25bn Over the Cambridge Analytica Lie

A New Mexico judge ordered Meta to pay $25bn on Wednesday, after a September jury found 43 million violations of the state's consumer protection law. At up to $5,000 a violation, it dwarfs the FTC's $5bn Facebook fine in 2019. Judge Francis Mathew wrote the harm "goes to the heart of Facebook's basic business proposition," and named Mark Zuckerberg directly. The money is meant for New Mexico schools, but Meta is appealing, arguing the violation count inflates the total. It lands on top of an $18bn multistate settlement in August. Meta shares barely moved.
