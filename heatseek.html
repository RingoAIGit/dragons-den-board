Anthropic Launches Opus 5 — Smaller, Cheaper, Outperforms Fable

July 24, 2026 · TechCrunch

Anthropic released Opus 5 on Friday, its newest heavyweight model that's both smaller and less expensive than Fable 5 while outperforming it on several benchmarks. The model is free from the 30-day data retention policy that covers Fable and Mythos, addressing privacy concerns.
