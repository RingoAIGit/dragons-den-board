Anthropic Closes In on a $7B Decart Acquisition, Beating Nvidia to the Table

Anthropic is closing in on its largest acquisition ever: a ~$7B deal for Israeli inference-efficiency startup Decart, Calcalist reports. Decart's founders and Sequoia, its biggest backer, chose Anthropic over a higher offer from Nvidia, which was in advanced talks. The deal, mostly paid in Anthropic shares, could be signed as soon as next month — ahead of the company's expected September IPO, with its prospectus already filed confidentially. Decart's tech wrings up to 8× more performance out of AI chips, a hedge as the industry shifts from training to inference. It would also hand Anthropic a development center in Israel.
