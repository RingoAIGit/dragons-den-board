A 20GB Model That Built a Flight Simulator on a Plane

Thinking Machines released Inkling 2 Mini on Thursday: 26.4 billion parameters, 3.4 billion active, Apache 2.0, about 20GB of weights. It runs on a 32GB MacBook or a DGX Spark.
