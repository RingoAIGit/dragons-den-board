South Korea Pioneers 'Lab-in-the-Loop' for AI-Driven Drug Discovery

The South Korean Ministry of Science and ICT has inaugurated an AI-powered drug discovery hub in Daegu, featuring a "Lab-in-the-Loop" system. This innovative platform connects AI models directly with automated laboratories and integrates data from two million patients to streamline and accelerate the drug development cycle. The initiative aims to reduce the time and cost associated with identifying and validating new drug candidates through a continuous feedback loop between AI predictions and real-world experimentation.
