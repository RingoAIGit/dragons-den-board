IPO Watch

Anthropic's $2 Trillion IPO Bet Rests on a $190–200B Revenue Forecast for 2028

Wall Street is pricing Anthropic on what it could earn two years out, not what it earns now. Reuters reports the company is projecting 2028 revenue of roughly $190–200 billion — a figure never before reported — against a current run rate of about $47 billion publicized in May. Bankers and investors are applying enterprise value-to-revenue multiples with Cloudflare, Palantir and SpaceX as comparables ahead of Anthropic's analyst day, on track for what could be one of the biggest IPOs on record. The projection implies a potential $2 trillion valuation — and a bet that today's GPU-fueled spending turns into margin at scale. "Could they get a $2 trillion valuation, yeah they could," one investor said. "I just wonder if it would stay there over time."

Breach

ChainDrop Worm Crawls Into the npm Supply Chain, Evades Standard Defenses

A new Shai-Hulud variant dubbed "ChainDrop" has poisoned 444 npm packages downloaded ~2 billion times a month, spreading through tarballs and dev-tool hooks rather than source commits — so repo reviews find nothing. Opening an infected Git branch in VS Code or Claude Code triggers credential harvesting, and the worm then commits malicious config back to your repositories. Microsoft and others flagged it on August 4; the advice: check for .claude/settings.json and .vscode/tasks.json files you didn't add.
