Japan Put Real Penalties on the Table for Frontier AI

A draft amendment to Japan's AI Promotion Act would make safety duties binding for frontier providers with enough local users: block cyberattack and weapons help, report serious harm, accept inspections. Nikkei reports fines up to ¥100 million, about $627,000. OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Alibaba and DeepSeek are all in scope. It's a bill for the 2027 Diet session, so nothing is in force yet.

Five Poisoned Models on Hugging Face, 2,428 Downloads

A scan of all 3.27 million public model repos found five GGUFs with backdoored chat templates.
