Security

Your Unfinished Model Weights Were Sitting on Someone Else's Cloud

Fireworks AI told customers on Monday that an October intrusion went much deeper than its first bulletin said. Attackers reached Fireworks' development machines through a third-party cloud provider on 23 October, then ran an unauthorised workload the next day. Billing addresses, invoice history and account balances were exposed, and for a limited set of accounts, secrets, datasets and unfinished model weights. Card numbers were never stored. Fireworks blames LLMJacking, the trade in stolen model access that Google's threat team says can bill a victim past $100,000 a day. Live inference and the Training SDK were untouched, and Notion has already pulled its connection.
