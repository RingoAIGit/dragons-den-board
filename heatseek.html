Marvell Buys the Lasers: $115bn for Lumentum, the Biggest Tech Deal Ever

Marvell agreed on Sunday to buy optical components maker Lumentum in an all-stock deal worth $115bn in enterprise value, with 4.5 Marvell shares per Lumentum share, a 23.9% premium to the pre-rumour close. Lumentum holders end up with roughly 33% of the combined company. The logic is bandwidth: lasers and optics are the scarce part of an AI network, and Marvell wants a full electrical-to-optical stack to take on Broadcom. Close date is the first half of 2028. Both stocks fell on the news.

OpenAI Offers to Switch Itself Off When the Grid Strains

OpenAI launched Grid Protectors, a national programme where future campuses cut power use on request. The Georgia pilot is a 25-year deal letting Georgia Power pull up to 1,000MW, roughly 700,000 homes' worth, and OpenAI pays full connection costs.
