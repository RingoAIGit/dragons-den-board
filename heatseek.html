This AI Learned Stratego — and Beat the World's Best Humans

Researchers at MIT, Carnegie Mellon, NYU and Stanford built Ataraxos, a system that thrashes top humans at Stratego, a board game built on hidden information. It beat the world's strongest player 15-1-4 and went 39-2 against championship contenders — while training far more cheaply than earlier methods. Published in Nature, it also generalised to Hanabi and other games, and the team says the approach could help with real-world hidden-information problems like negotiations and cybersecurity.
