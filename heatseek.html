US–China Decoupling

Meta Unwinds Its US$2B Manus AI Deal After Beijing Block

South China Morning Post · August 12, 2026

Meta is fully unwinding its acquisition of Chinese-founded AI agent platform Manus — three months after Beijing blocked the deal on national-security grounds — capping off another high-profile AI decoupling between Washington and Beijing.

Manus told users Tuesday it will delete data generated by “certain users” since December 29, when Meta acquired the startup, as it transitions back to independent operations. Users have until August 23 to back up their data; the deletion is needed “to comply with regulatory requirements in specific parts of the world.” Manus’s parent Butterfly Effect was founded in 2022 with offices in Beijing and Wuhan, then re-incorporated as a Singapore company last summer.

Regulation

Every Claude Sentence Gets an Invisible Watermark — Worldwide, Thanks to the EU

Euronews · August 11, 2026

Anthropic has begun weaving imperceptible watermarks into all Claude text output, and attaching signed provenance metadata to generated files — rolling out globally from August 2 to meet the EU AI Act’s Article 50 transparency rules, which carry fines up to €15M or 3% of turnover.

The mark survives copying, pasting and light editing, and applies across Claude, Claude Code, Claude Cowork and the API, even via AWS or Google Cloud. Anthropic is candid about limits: a watermark signals Claude processed the text, not that it authored it — and heavy rewriting or translation can strip it out.
