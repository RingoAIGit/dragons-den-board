AI Commerce

ChatGPT Can Now Pay for the Thing It Just Found For You

OpenAI launched ChatGPT Wallet on Friday: add a card once, and its "dots" agents can complete a checkout for you. Every purchase needs a biometric approval (Face ID or a fingerprint), and the network hands over a payment token instead of your card number, so the agent and the shop never see the real details. It is US-only, Pro subscribers only, 18 and over, and limited to Amex, Chase, Mastercard and Visa. OpenAI says eventual plans are to let you pre-approve spending limits and categories. The catch: plenty of retailers still block shopping agents outright.

Infrastructure

Old Routers Grounded Every Flight in America for Five Hours

The FAA ordered a nationwide ground stop on Friday afternoon after communications and radar data went inconsistent across air traffic control centres. Nearly 10,000 flights were cancelled and roughly 25,000 delayed, the longest shutdown since 2001. Transport Secretary Sean Duffy blamed 2001-era switches and routers overloaded during a contractor's copper-to-fibre upgrade. Homeland Security says no sign of an attack, and the FAA administrator ruled out anything AI-related. Backlogs ran into Saturday.
