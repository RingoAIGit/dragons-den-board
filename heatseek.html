Claude Cracked a 25-Year NSA Mystery in Under Half an Hour

Anthropic published two cryptography results on Friday, both produced by Claude working largely on its own. Given the NIST elliptic-curve seeds the NSA handed over in 2000 with no explanation, Claude took under 30 minutes to trace them to lrand48 — an old C library random-number generator seeded from a Unix timestamp. P-256's published seed turned up at iteration 1,842. It is not a break: it makes a hidden NSA backdoor much less plausible, and Bitcoin's secp256k1 was never in the frame. The companion paper has Claude redesigning quantum circuits, including one that needs 17x fewer gates.
